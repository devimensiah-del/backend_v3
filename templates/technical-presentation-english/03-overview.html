01 — System Overview

What is IMENSIAH?

IMENSIAH transforms basic company information into comprehensive strategic reports using multiple AI models. The system collects public data, enriches it with real-time macroeconomic context, and applies 11 globally recognized strategic analysis frameworks.

A process that traditionally takes weeks of consultant work is executed in minutes, combining Google Gemini's real-time research capability with Claude Opus's analytical depth.

Why is it impressive?
The system orchestrates the most advanced AI models of 2025 in a sophisticated pipeline: Gemini 3.0 Pro for web-enabled intelligence gathering, and Claude Opus 4.5 for deep strategic reasoning—delivering enterprise-grade analysis at unprecedented speed.
